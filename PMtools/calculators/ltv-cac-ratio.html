LTV : CAC Ratio Calculator

Check whether your unit economics work by comparing lifetime value to acquisition cost.

LTV : CAC ratio 4.0 : 1 Healthy — at or above the 3:1 benchmark

FORMULA LTV:CAC = LTV ÷ CAC. ~3:1 is the common health benchmark; much higher can mean under-investment in growth.

What it tells you

The single fastest read on whether growth is sustainable. Below 3:1, acquisition is likely too expensive; far above 3:1, you may be leaving growth on the table by under-spending.
