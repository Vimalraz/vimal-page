Superhuman's PMF Engine

How Superhuman turned the fuzzy idea of product-market fit into a measurable, improvable system.

Context

Superhuman struggled to know if they had product-market fit, so they made it measurable.

The key decision

They surveyed users: 'How would you feel if you could no longer use Superhuman?' and tracked the % who said 'very disappointed.' They focused the roadmap on converting the 'somewhat disappointed' segment.

Outcome

The metric climbed past the 40% benchmark, and the method became a widely-copied PMF playbook.

Lessons

  • You can instrument PMF, not just feel it.
  • Segment your users and improve the ones on the edge.
  • A single recurring question can anchor a roadmap.
