ICE Scoring

A lightweight prioritization score from Impact, Confidence, and Ease — faster than RICE for quick calls.

When to use

  • Ranking growth experiments quickly
  • Low-stakes, high-volume decisions

When not to use

  • Cross-team roadmap planning where effort varies wildly

Inputs

  • Impact (1–10)
  • Confidence (1–10)
  • Ease (1–10)

Steps

  • Score 1–10 on each
  • Average (or sum) the three
  • Rank descending

Outputs

  • A fast experiment backlog ranking

Common mistakes

  • Conflating Ease with Effort
  • Anchoring all scores around 7

Example

Onboarding tooltip: Impact 6, Confidence 8, Ease 9 → 7.7. Beats a risky pricing test at 4.3.
