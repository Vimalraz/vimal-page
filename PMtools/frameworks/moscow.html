MoSCoW Prioritization

Scope a release by sorting requirements into Must, Should, Could, and Won't-have-now.

When to use

  • Scoping a fixed-deadline release
  • Aligning on what's actually required

When not to use

  • Ranking a long continuous backlog

Inputs

  • A list of candidate requirements

Steps

  • Sort each into Must / Should / Could / Won't
  • Cap Musts to fit the timebox
  • Revisit Won'ts next cycle

Outputs

  • A scoped, agreed release

Common mistakes

  • Everything becomes a Must
  • No Won't list to manage expectations

Example

An MVP launch with 4 Musts shipped on time; 6 Coulds moved to v1.1.
