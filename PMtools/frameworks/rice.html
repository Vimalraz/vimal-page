RICE Scoring

Prioritize initiatives by Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort with a single comparable score.

When to use

  • Comparing many initiatives objectively
  • You need a defensible ranking to align stakeholders

When not to use

  • A single obvious must-do bet
  • Strategy-level bets where conviction beats arithmetic

Inputs

  • Reach (users/quarter)
  • Impact (0.25 minimal → 3 massive)
  • Confidence (%)
  • Effort (person-months)

Steps

  • Score each factor per item
  • Compute (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort
  • Sort descending
  • Sanity-check the top — does the order feel right?

Outputs

  • A ranked backlog
  • A RICE score per item you can defend

Common mistakes

  • False precision — treat scores as directional
  • Gaming confidence to push pet projects
  • Ignoring strategic fit entirely

Example

A 12-item backlog: Feature B scores 42 (high reach, low effort) and beats Feature A at 18 — surfacing a non-obvious winner.
