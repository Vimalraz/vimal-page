How to Choose a North Star Metric

Pick and pressure-test the single metric that reflects the value your product delivers.

What a North Star is (and isn't)

It's the single metric that best captures the value customers get from your product. It is not revenue — revenue is the result of delivering value, not the value itself.

Three tests for a good North Star

  1. Value test — does it go up when customers genuinely succeed?
  2. Leading test — does it predict future revenue/retention?
  3. Actionable test — can teams actually move it?

Decompose it into inputs

A North Star you can't break into inputs is a poster, not a system. Spotify's "time spent listening" decomposes into activation, sessions per user, and session length — each ownable by a team.

Add guardrails

Every North Star can be gamed. Pair it with guardrail metrics (e.g. unsubscribe rate, support tickets) so teams don't win the metric while losing the customer.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a vanity number (page views).
  • Choosing a lagging result (ARR) with no inputs.
  • Changing it every quarter — it should be stable for years.
