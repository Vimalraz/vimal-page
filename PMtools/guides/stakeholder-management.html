Stakeholder Management for PMs

Build trust and alignment across leadership, engineering, design, and go-to-market.

Map before you manage

List your stakeholders and rate each on influence and interest. You manage a high-influence skeptic very differently from a supportive bystander.

No surprises

The core rule of stakeholder trust: leaders should never hear bad news first in a public forum. Pre-wire decisions one-on-one.

Translate, don't transmit

The same update should sound different to a CFO (cost, risk) and an engineer (scope, tradeoffs). Tailor the framing, keep the facts identical.

Disagree and commit, visibly

When a call goes against you, support it publicly. Re-litigating in the hallway destroys trust faster than any wrong decision.
