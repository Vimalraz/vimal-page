Quarterly Planning Playbook

A repeatable four-week cadence to go from messy inputs to a committed, aligned quarterly plan.

The cadence

A good quarterly plan is built, not announced. Run it as a four-week process so the plan arrives with buy-in already baked in.

Week 1 — Inputs

Gather the raw material: last quarter's results, the current strategy, customer signals, and the top asks from sales, support, and leadership. Don't filter yet — just collect.

Week 2 — Drafts

Write a draft set of objectives and the candidate initiatives under each. Score the initiatives with RICE to get a defensible starting order. Share the draft async for comment.

Week 3 — Alignment

Pre-wire the plan one-on-one with each key stakeholder before any group meeting. Surface disagreements privately. By the time you present, there should be no surprises.

Week 4 — Commit

Lock objectives and key results, name owners, and publish. Capacity-check: if everything is a priority, nothing is. Cut to fit.

Anti-patterns

  • Planning in a single marathon meeting.
  • A plan with more objectives than the team can hold in its head.
  • Treating the roadmap as a delivery contract instead of a statement of intent.
