About The Agency

From ₹3,500
to South India's
Finest Agency

Partner With Us

Started in 2015 with just ₹3,500, The InterMentalist has today grown into South India's finest influencer marketing agency, partnering with almost every brand that serves customers at a pan-India or global level.

The agency works closely with the South Indian film industry and the entire creator ecosystem across the region — strategising, identifying the right people, drafting scripts, and providing pre- and post-production services along with complete analysis and statistics.

Over the decade, the agency has worked with 200+ pan-Indian and global brands, 400+ premium actors, influencers and creators from the South Indian digital space. They are one of the most aggressive and result-driven players in the game.

The InterMentalist has played a defining role in shaping the South's creator economy — building sustainable models for influencer marketing and digital movie promotions with efficient processes and deep cultural understanding.

At its core lies a rare strength: the ability to influence the influencers — a power that has made it the most preferred partner for brands and agencies seeking to win in South India.
